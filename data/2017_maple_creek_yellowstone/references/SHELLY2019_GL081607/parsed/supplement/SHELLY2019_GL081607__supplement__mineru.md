## Introduction

Supplemental material for this manuscript includes three earthquake catalogs derived in this study (text files), three supplemental figures and three animations showing swarm evolution (MP4 movie files).

Figure S3. Event magnitudes. a) Frequency-magnitude distribution for the swarm. Gutenberg-Richter b value is estimated via maximum likelihood [Tinti and Mulargia, 1987]. b) Event magnitudes with time. Red dots show events in University of Utah Seismograph Stations catalog. Blue dots show newly detected events. Gray line shows total cumulative events with time. Black line shows cumulative events at or above the cutoff magnitude of -0.5 (black dashed line). c) Evolution of the Gutenberg-Richter b value over the course of the swarm. Black line shows b value using a centered moving window of 1000 events above a cutoff magnitude (M ) of -0.5. Blue horizontal lines periodically show the time range of events to include 1000 events. The b value is corrected for detection saturation using the technique described by Shelly et al. [2016a], but this correction is insignificant for most of this swarm (compare with light gray line).
