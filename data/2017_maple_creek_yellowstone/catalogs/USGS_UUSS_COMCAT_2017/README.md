### Native schema and local rows

The ComCat CSV preserves service fields:

    time, latitude, longitude, depth, mag, magType, nst, gap, dmin, rms,
    net, id, updated, place, type, horizontalError, depthError, magError,
    magNst, status, locationSource, magSource

The full snapshot has 9 event rows plus a header over the requested
2017-06-11 through 2017-09-15 scan span. The frozen benchmark snapshot has 5
event rows plus a header. All rows are network uu, status reviewed, and
location/magnitude source uu.

The unexpectedly small 5-row result is a property of this archived ComCat
snapshot/query, not evidence that the Maple Creek swarm contained only five
events. The article reports nearly 2,500 routinely detected events, and the
high-resolution products target much larger populations. Treat this baseline
as a reproducibility anchor for the exact query, not as a completeness
benchmark.

### Benchmark use

- Use for checking event-ID/time/location interoperability and for a
  conservative operational baseline.
- Do not use it as the primary event truth set, completeness estimate, or
  substitute for the UUSS internal routine catalog used by Shelly and Pang.
- Keep the ComCat query, source fields, retrieval timestamp, and checksums with
  any scoring run.
