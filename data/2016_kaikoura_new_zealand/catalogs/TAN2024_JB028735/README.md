# Tan et al. (2024) SUGAR catalog

The benchmark-required supplementary package is complete locally: the SI PDF and Tables S09–S12 are under `references/TAN2024_JB028735/supplement/`. The publisher also lists a Movie SI-S01; that optional visualization file is not needed for catalog extraction or benchmark preparation and is not currently archived.

- `source_ref`: `TAN2024_JB028735`

Source: Tan et al. (2024), DOI [10.1029/2024JB028735](https://doi.org/10.1029/2024JB028735).

## Catalog products

- `TAN2024_JB028735__catalog_sugar_S10.xlsx` — Table S10, the 67,660-event SUGAR catalog for 13 November–31 December 2016 (48.5 days).
- `TAN2024_JB028735__catalog_sugar_relocated_S11.xlsx` — Table S11, the 46,440-event Growclust-relocated subset before the final cluster-size filtering described in the paper.
- `SUGAR_associated_phases.zip` and extracted `phases/` — 1,172 phase files associated with the SUGAR catalog, downloaded from Zenodo [10.5281/zenodo.10937462](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10937462).

The paper reports a final quality-controlled set of 41,392 events after retaining clusters with at least 10 events. Table S11 is the relocated intermediate product; the supporting files currently available from Wiley do not include a separate 41,392-row final CSV. The original S10/S11 XLSX files remain under `references/TAN2024_JB028735/supplement/` for provenance.
