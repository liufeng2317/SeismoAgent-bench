## 1 1. Description of the supplementary materials

• Figure S1: Waveform of a detected earthquake recorded on the vertical component of station JOKA and filtered between 2 and 20 Hz (black line). The red line corresponds to the kurtosis, the function F(t) computed between 0.5 and 1.5 seconds. The blue line is the apriori information on the arrival of the P-wave, (w(t)) that is centered at 1.0s. This a-priori information is based on the P-wave pick of the template event that detected this earthquake. The green curve shows $F _ { w } ( t )$ that is the product of F and w and the peak of this function gives an estimate of the P-wave arrival time, here at 1.11 s as marked by the dashed line
