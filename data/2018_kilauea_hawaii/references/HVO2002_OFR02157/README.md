# HVO2002_OFR02157

Nakata, J. S. (compiler), 2002. Hawaiian Volcano Observatory Summary 101,
Part I, Seismic Data, January to December 2001. U.S. Geological Survey
Open-File Report 02-157. https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2002/0157/pdf/of02-157.pdf
(downloaded 2026-10-23; SHA-256 `4c29decc6aec2fb70059d723e17befe1a17639efff10d533a925886c2989974d`).

Role in this case: source of the HVO operational HYPOINVERSE crustal model
(the Klein linear-gradient model family; Matoza et al. 2021 and HVO locate
with it): "specified by velocities at four depth points ... linear
interpolation between points ... homogeneous half-space": 1.9 km/s at 0 km,
6.5 at 4.6 km, 6.9 at 15.0 km, 8.3 at >= 16.5 km (PDF text page 19).
Registered as `hvo_klein_linear` in `data/models/velocity_models.json`.
